# CyberMeters Product Constitution v1.0

## Vision

CyberMeters exists to become the leading **External Exposure Intelligence Platform** for small and medium-sized businesses.

We do not try to become every cybersecurity product.

We become exceptionally good at one category.

---

## Mission

Help organizations discover, understand, monitor and reduce everything attackers can see from the outside before it is exploited.

Everything we build must answer one question:

> **Does this improve our ability to identify, explain or reduce external exposure?**

If the answer is **no**, we do not build it.

---

# Product Pillars

## 1. Attack Surface Intelligence

Discover and continuously monitor:

- Domains
- DNS
- SSL/TLS
- Certificates
- Subdomains
- Cloud assets
- Public storage
- Admin surfaces
- Internet-facing services
- Historical changes

---

## 2. Business Email Intelligence

Continuously evaluate the external email ecosystem.

Includes:

- SPF
- DKIM
- DMARC
- MTA-STS
- TLS-RPT
- MX infrastructure
- Email provider posture
- Mail routing
- Email exposure
- Business Email Exposure Score

### Future Evolution

- Microsoft 365
- Google Workspace
- Business Email Compromise Risk Monitoring
- Business Email Compromise Protection

---

## 3. Identity Intelligence

Monitor identity-related exposure without becoming an identity provider.

Examples:

- Microsoft 365 posture
- Entra ID posture
- Google Workspace posture
- MFA visibility
- OAuth applications
- Mail forwarding rules
- Legacy authentication
- Identity exposure trends

---

## 4. Brand Intelligence

Protect the customer's public identity.

Includes:

- Typosquatting
- Homoglyph domains
- Lookalike domains
- Certificate abuse
- Brand impersonation
- Domain registration monitoring
- Public phishing infrastructure detection

---

## 5. Executive Intelligence

Transform technical findings into business decisions.

Provide:

- Cyber Score
- Business Email Exposure Score
- Executive dashboards
- Historical trends
- Executive PDF reports
- Business impact explanations
- Prioritized remediation guidance

# Design Principles

- Everything external.
- Everything continuously monitored.
- Everything explained.
- Everything actionable.

---

# What CyberMeters Is Not

CyberMeters is **NOT**:

- A SIEM
- An EDR
- An XDR
- A SOAR platform
- A vulnerability scanner like Nessus
- A penetration testing tool
- A ticketing system
- A compliance management suite
- A GRC platform

---

# Product Boundaries

The following are **not** standalone CyberMeters products:

- Vendor Risk
- Supply Chain Risk
- Cyber Essentials
- ISO 27001 Management
- Broad Compliance Management

If they remain, they exist only as supporting intelligence or optional reporting derived from our core exposure data.

# Customer Promise

CyberMeters helps organizations answer five essential questions:

1. What can attackers see?
2. Can attackers impersonate us?
3. Is our identity exposed?
4. Is our brand being abused?
5. What should we fix first?

---

# Engineering Rule

Every proposed feature must satisfy at least one of the following:

- Improves external visibility.
- Improves exposure monitoring.
- Improves Business Email Intelligence.
- Improves Identity Intelligence.
- Improves Brand Intelligence.
- Improves Executive Intelligence.
- Improves customer experience.

If it does not, it does not belong in CyberMeters.
