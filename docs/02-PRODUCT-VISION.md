# CyberMeters Product Vision

## The External Exposure Intelligence Platform

CyberMeters is the External Exposure Intelligence Platform that continuously discovers, monitors, explains, validates, and helps reduce external cyber exposure.

CyberMeters helps organisations understand what attackers can see from the outside, what should be fixed first, and whether remediation has been successful.

---

## Our Intelligence Pillars

### 1. Attack Surface Intelligence

Discover internet-facing assets, services, cloud resources, certificates, technologies, and external attack paths.

### 2. Business Email Intelligence

Monitor email authentication, domain spoofing risk, Microsoft 365 posture, Google Workspace posture, and the external factors that contribute to Business Email Compromise risk.

### 3. Identity Intelligence

Monitor externally observable identity risks including authentication exposure, identity infrastructure, tenant posture, executive account exposure, and identity-related attack paths.

### 4. Brand Intelligence

Monitor lookalike domains, typosquatting, phishing infrastructure, brand abuse, and public impersonation attempts.

### 5. Executive Intelligence

Protect executives by identifying external threats targeting key individuals, executive impersonation risks, exposed executive assets, and reputation-related attack vectors.

---

## What CyberMeters Enables

CyberMeters transforms intelligence into action through:

- Continuous Exposure Monitoring
- Risk Prioritisation
- Actionable Remediation Guidance
- Security Hardening Assessments
- Continuous Security Validation

Security Hardening Assessments are not a separate product pillar. They are a premium capability built on top of CyberMeters intelligence.

CyberMeters discovers the problem, explains the problem, guides the fix, and validates the fix.

---

## Product Philosophy

Security teams do not need more dashboards.

They need better intelligence.

Every feature developed for CyberMeters must satisfy at least one of the following objectives:

- Discover external cyber exposure.
- Explain why the exposure matters.
- Prioritise what should be fixed first.
- Guide organisations through remediation.
- Validate that remediation was successful.

If a proposed feature does not strengthen one or more of the five intelligence pillars or support this intelligence lifecycle, it should not become part of CyberMeters.

---

## What CyberMeters Is Not

CyberMeters is not:

- A SIEM
- An EDR
- A Managed SOC
- A GRC Platform
- A Vendor Risk Platform
- A Supply Chain Risk Platform
- A Compliance Management Platform

CyberMeters remains focused on one mission:

**Helping organisations understand, reduce, and continuously monitor their external cyber exposure.**
